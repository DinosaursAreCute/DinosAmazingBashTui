              are medians of other/base with a bootstrap CI. "≈ same" = gap below the noise floor or < 5%; "unclear" = CI spans 1.
flags:        ~ noisy (CV > 25%)   ° outlier rounds present (kept, never dropped)   k/n ok = rounds where the expected text appeared   stale! =
